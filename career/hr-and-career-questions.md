# HR & Career Questions

## Why are you looking for a new opportunity?

Never criticize your employer.

> I’ve learned a lot at SmartCrowd over the past 3.5 years, and I’m grateful for the opportunities I had there, including my promotion and the chance to work on a regulated fintech product.
>
> At this stage of my career, I’m looking for a role where I can contribute more as a full-stack engineer, work on larger technical challenges, and continue growing while collaborating with global teams.

---

## What are you looking for?

> I’m looking for a remote full-stack engineering role where I can continue working with React, TypeScript, Node.js, and modern backend technologies while taking on more ownership. I also want to keep growing in areas like cloud and DevOps because I enjoy understanding the complete software development lifecycle — from building features to deploying and monitoring them in production.

---

## Why are you learning DevOps?

Don’t say: “I’m learning DevOps.” (passive)

> Working on both frontend and backend made me realize that building an application is only part of the job. I wanted to understand how software is built, tested, deployed, and monitored in production. That’s why I started applying DevOps practices in my personal projects — Docker, GitHub Actions, CI/CD, and deployment automation. My goal isn’t to become a DevOps engineer, but to become a better full-stack engineer who understands the complete development lifecycle.

---

## Strengths

Pick 3–4 and give a one-line proof for each:

- Ownership
- Quick learner
- Cross-functional collaboration
- Product thinking
- Full-stack mindset

Example:

> One of my strengths is ownership. When I take a feature like registration or STF, I don’t stop at UI — I follow through on API integration, edge cases, and delivery with product and design.

---

## Weakness

Avoid fake weaknesses (“I work too hard”) and avoid “I can’t communicate.”

> Earlier in my career, I sometimes focused too much on implementing solutions instead of discussing ideas early with teammates. I’ve improved this by communicating more frequently with product managers and engineers during development, which has helped reduce misunderstandings and improve delivery.

---
