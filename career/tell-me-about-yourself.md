# Tell Me About Yourself

Most important answer in a recruiter screen. Aim for **90 seconds to 2 minutes**.

## Structure

```text
Who you are
    ↓
Current company + growth
    ↓
Biggest achievements
    ↓
Leadership / collaboration
    ↓
Side projects + DevOps growth
    ↓
What you’re looking for now
```

Avoid: listing every technology. Prefer: business impact + ownership.

Use verbs like: **led, owned, designed, implemented, redesigned, collaborated, optimized, delivered**.

---

## Polished version (use this flow)

> Hi, I'm S.M. Hazrat Ali. I'm a Software Engineer with around 3.5 years of professional experience building modern web applications using React, TypeScript, Node.js, and Express.
>
> I joined SmartCrowd, a Dubai-based fintech company, as a Junior Software Engineer and was promoted to Software Engineer within two years based on my contributions to frontend architecture and feature delivery.
>
> During my time there, I've worked on core business features such as investor registration, the Share Transfer Facility, multilingual support, and frontend performance optimization. I've also contributed to backend API development, collaborated closely with product managers and designers, and mentored junior developers through code reviews and technical guidance.
>
> Outside of my day-to-day work, I enjoy building full-stack applications to deepen my technical skills. Recently, I've developed projects such as an AI-powered SQL Assistant, a job platform, and a healthcare appointment system. I've also been expanding into DevOps by implementing CI/CD pipelines with GitHub Actions, Dockerizing my applications, and learning deployment and cloud practices because I enjoy understanding the complete software delivery lifecycle.
>
> At this stage of my career, I'm looking for an opportunity where I can contribute as a full-stack engineer, continue strengthening my DevOps skills, work with global teams, and take ownership of impactful products.

**Don’t memorize word-for-word — remember the flow.**

## Shorter 60–90s version (if they interrupt)

> I'm a Software Engineer with about 3.5 years of experience. I work remotely at SmartCrowd, a Dubai-based fintech platform, where I was promoted from Junior to Software Engineer within two years. I've owned frontend work on investor registration, Share Transfer Facility, RTL support, and performance improvements, and I've also contributed to Node.js APIs. Outside work I build full-stack projects and CI/CD pipelines. I'm looking for a remote full-stack role with greater ownership and global collaboration.

## Common mistakes to avoid

| Don’t say | Say instead |
|-----------|-------------|
| “I know React, Node…” | “I build products using…” / “My primary expertise is…” |
| “I helped my team…” | “I led / owned / delivered…” |
| “I want to learn new technologies” (as the ending) | End with what you **bring**, then growth |
| Too many framework names in the intro | Business impact first; tech second |

---

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Start with role + years of experience
- [ ] Mention promotion early
- [ ] Name 2–3 concrete features (registration, STF, RTL)
- [ ] Mention mentoring / collaboration briefly
- [ ] Mention 1–2 side projects + DevOps
- [ ] End with target role (full-stack, remote, ownership)
- [ ] Speak slowly; finish under ~2 minutes
