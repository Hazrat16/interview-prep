# Interview Tips (Recruiter / ORC)

## Mindset

You’re not proving you’re the smartest engineer in the room.

You’re convincing them they can **confidently introduce you to clients**.

Your strengths to lean on:

- ~3.5 years experience
- Internal promotion
- International remote work
- Regulated fintech experience
- Full-stack direction + DevOps growth
- Mentoring / ownership
- Strong personal projects

---

## Likely call flow (~30–60 min)

```text
Introduction (5 min)
      ↓
Your experience (10 min)
      ↓
SmartCrowd deep dive (15 min)
      ↓
Projects (10 min)
      ↓
Career goals (5 min)
      ↓
Your questions (5 min)
```

---

## Communication

- Speak a little slower; pauses are fine
- Structure answers: Context → Your role → Challenge → Result
- Prefer ownership language over “I helped…”
- Don’t bluff. Better:

> I haven’t worked directly with that technology yet, but here’s a similar problem I’ve solved…

---

## Answer formula for experience questions

```text
Business context
→ Your responsibility
→ Hard part
→ What you did
→ Result / approval / impact
```

---

## CV talking tips

- Explain SmartCrowd as a **business**, then your role
- Prefer defendable impact over vague percentages
- Have 2 “best projects”: one production feature + one personal full-stack project
- DevOps: say what you’ve **implemented** (CI/CD, Docker), not only what you’re “learning”

---
