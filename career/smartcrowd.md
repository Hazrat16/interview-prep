# SmartCrowd Deep Dive

## What SmartCrowd is (business first)

SmartCrowd is a **Dubai-based regulated fintech platform** for **fractional real estate investment**.

Investors can put in as little as AED 500 and own a fractional stake in income-generating property, instead of buying an entire unit. The platform is regulated (DFSA / DIFC context) and handles onboarding, KYC, investment, portfolio, and transactions.

### Simple investor story

```text
Investor registers
      ↓
KYC / AML verification
      ↓
Browses properties
      ↓
Invests from AED 500+
      ↓
Property reaches 100% funding
      ↓
SPV owns the property
      ↓
Investors own shares in the SPV
      ↓
Rental income distributed
      ↓
Exit via sale or Share Transfer Facility (STF)
```
