## About section (story style — recommended)

I’m a Software Engineer with 3.5 years of experience building fintech products and full-stack web applications for global users.

I started my career at SmartCrowd, a Dubai-based fintech company, where I joined as a Junior Software Engineer and was promoted to Software Engineer within two years. Since then, I’ve been building products that thousands of investors rely on, collaborating with product managers, designers, and engineers to deliver scalable, user-focused solutions.

While my primary expertise is React, TypeScript, and modern frontend architecture, I’ve gradually expanded into backend development with Node.js, Express, MongoDB, and PostgreSQL. I enjoy understanding how systems work end-to-end, from designing intuitive user experiences to building APIs and improving application performance.

Some of the work I’m most proud of includes:

• Leading the frontend implementation of a redesigned investor registration flow to meet new regulatory requirements from DIFC, while maintaining compatibility for existing users and delivering the project within a tight deadline.

• Driving frontend architecture improvements, performance optimization, reusable component development, and multilingual (RTL) support for a regulated fintech platform.

• Building full-stack applications outside of work, including an AI-powered SQL Assistant, a job platform, a healthcare appointment system, and real-time applications to continuously improve my engineering skills.

Lately, I’ve also been investing time in DevOps and cloud practices. I’ve been implementing CI/CD pipelines with GitHub Actions, Dockerizing applications, and learning deployment and monitoring because I believe understanding the complete software delivery lifecycle makes me a better software engineer.

I enjoy solving real business problems, taking ownership of features, mentoring teammates, and continuously learning. Whether it’s improving an existing product or building something from scratch, I’m always excited by opportunities to create software that delivers meaningful impact.

I’m always happy to connect with engineers, product leaders, recruiters, and anyone passionate about building great products.

## About section tips

- Answer **who you are as an engineer**, not a dump of every technology
- Mention promotion, fintech, remote/global work, ownership
- Use a few proud bullets (compliance registration, architecture/RTL, side projects)
- Avoid generic AI tone and “If you are looking for a frontend developer…” freelance closers
- Skills belong in Skills/Experience; About should tell a story
