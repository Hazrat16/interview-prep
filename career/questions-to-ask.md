# Questions to Ask Them

Always ask questions. It shows long-term thinking.

## Recommended for Talvette / talent platforms

1. What types of international clients do you typically work with?
2. Based on my profile, what kinds of roles do you think would be the best fit?
3. How does the matching process work after this conversation?
4. What skills are currently most in demand among your clients?
5. Is there anything in my experience or CV that I should strengthen to improve my chances with your clients?
6. What does a strong candidate look like for the roles you place most often?

## Optional follow-ups

- Typical engagement model: full-time vs contract?
- Common timezones / overlap expectations for remote roles?
- How soon after this conversation might clients reach out?

## Avoid

- Only asking about salary in the first question set
- Questions you could answer from their website in 30 seconds
- “Do you have any jobs for me?” without context — better to ask about fit and process
