# Career & Recruiter Screen Prep

Notes for Open Role Conversations (ORC), recruiter screens, and career storytelling — separate from technical topic drills.

## What an Open Role Conversation is

Not a coding interview for a specific job.

A 30–60 minute call to:

- understand your experience
- understand what roles you want
- decide whether they can recommend you to clients

**Communication is ~70% of the evaluation.**

Recruiters silently ask:

- Can this person communicate clearly?
- Can I confidently present them to a client?
- Do they understand what they’ve built?
- Are they honest about their contributions?
- Would a client enjoy working with them?
